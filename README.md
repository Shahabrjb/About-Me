### Ongoing Research — Adaptive RAN Anomaly Detection

I am developing an anomaly-detection framework to identify and rank **unbalanced cellular sectors whose correction is expected to produce the highest improvement in downlink spectral efficiency**.

The current research direction combines:

- **Isolation Forest** for unsupervised anomaly detection
- **LSTM-based modelling** to estimate an adaptive contamination rate from temporal KPI behaviour
- impact-aware ranking based on expected spectral-efficiency improvement
- comparison against fixed-contamination baselines

The research goal is not simply to detect unusual sectors, but to identify the anomalies that are **most operationally valuable to fix**.

[View the research overview](projects/adaptive-ran-anomaly-detection/README.md)
